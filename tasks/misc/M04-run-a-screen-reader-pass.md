AccessKit publishes the metadata, but no screen reader has ever been pointed at the editor.
Walk the whole interface with one per platform — Narrator, VoiceOver, Orca — and record what is
unreadable, unlabelled, or unreachable.

Blocked on the assistive technology itself; the fixes it finds belong in follow-up tasks.

## Acceptance

A recorded pass per platform, with each defect it finds filed and the checklist limit replaced by
what was actually observed.
