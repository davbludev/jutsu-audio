Create generator inputs/outputs, seed derivation, algorithm version, parameter schema, provenance, and replace/regenerate rules.

## Acceptance

Same recipe/version/assets produce byte-identical command output and deterministic render.

## Notes

- 2026-08-19: the contract is `crates/jutsu-audio-extensions/src/recipe.rs`, written up in
  `docs/design/jutsu-audio-generator-recipe-v1.md`. A recipe is generator type, algorithm version,
  seed, length and parameters — and nothing else may affect the output.
- 2026-08-19: entity IDs are derived from the recipe's identity rather than freshly generated, so
  running a recipe twice produces byte-identical *commands*, not just identical audio, and
  `Replace` can find what it replaced without a side table.
- 2026-08-19: seed derivation and identity hashing are specified here (splitmix64 over FNV-1a)
  rather than borrowed from a standard hasher whose output may change between releases.
- 2026-08-19: generated audio is never stored. The mix runs the generator from the asset's
  provenance, which is why a project can never disagree with its own recipe. `AudioAssetSource::
  Generated` gained a `parameters` field, omitted when empty so existing files round-trip byte for
  byte — the golden fixture proves it.
