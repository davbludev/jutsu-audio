Support loop playback, time display, keyboard seeking, markers, and sample-accurate render positions.

## Acceptance

Loop boundaries and seeks remain consistent between GUI, CLI, playback, and export.

## Notes

- 2026-08-19: `markers` and `loop_region` are additive optional members of the project, skipped
  when empty, so nothing about existing files or the schema version changes.
- 2026-08-19: the renderer wraps by rendering in segments that stop exactly on the loop end, so
  the wrap lands on a frame rather than on a device block boundary. Loop bounds live in the
  transport as two atomics (`loop_end == 0` means "not looping"), keeping the callback allocation
  free and lock free.
- 2026-08-19: the same region bounds an export — `export_wav` with `use_loop_region`, and the
  editor's Export WAV when a loop is active — so playback and both export paths agree frame for
  frame.
- 2026-08-19: GUI keys are L (loop), M (marker at playhead), `,` / `.` (previous/next marker),
  Home/End. Markers and the loop band are drawn on the ruler; a disabled loop is drawn fainter
  rather than hidden.
