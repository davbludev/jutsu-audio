Model tempo/time-signature changes and stable conversion among samples, seconds, bars, beats, and ticks.

## Acceptance

Boundary/round-trip tests cover changes and both interfaces share formatting semantics.

## Notes

- 2026-08-19: `crates/jutsu-audio-model/src/tempo.rs` holds the tempo map and every conversion —
  frames, seconds, beats, and `bar.beat.tick` at 960 ticks per beat. `tempo` on the project is
  additive and omitted when empty; an empty map is 120 BPM in 4/4, so a project that never
  mentions tempo still has one.
- 2026-08-19: the semantics that make position and frame exact inverses: a tempo or signature
  change *begins a bar*, so a part-bar before it counts as a short bar. Round-trip tests cover
  both directions across tempo and signature changes, and quantisation at the boundaries.
- 2026-08-19: both interfaces share the formatting. The editor's status bar and the CLI's
  `convert_time` call the same `MusicalPosition::format`, and `set_tempo_map` is the one way to
  change it.
