Add track/bus/master graph routing with cycle validation, channel-layout rules, metering, and click-free control changes.

## Acceptance

Routing persists and renders consistently in real-time/offline.

## Notes

- 2026-08-19: the mix is now routed rather than flat: clips into a track buffer, the track's strip
  (gain, pan, mute/solo) applied, then into its output bus; buses fold from the leaves inward into
  the master. Bus and track strips read the same parameter keys a clip does, so one name means one
  thing everywhere.
- 2026-08-19: `Project::bus_route_loops` and `ValidationCode::BusCycle` reject a routing loop at
  validation, which is what keeps the fold guaranteed to terminate.
- 2026-08-19: `mix_project_metered` reports the peak each track and bus contributed. Static, not
  live — it describes the rendered mix, which is what a level check wants; the transport still
  reports what is playing now.
- 2026-08-19: publishing a new mix during playback now crossfades over 240 frames instead of
  swapping outright, so a gain or routing change while playing no longer steps the waveform. A
  seek cancels the fade — blending across a jump would mix two unrelated moments.
- 2026-08-19: commands for routing and strips: `AddBus`, `RemoveBus`, `SetBusOutput`,
  `SetTrackOutput`, `SetTrackParameter`, `SetBusParameter`, each with an inverse. The GUI and CLI
  surfaces for them are 01.06.05.
