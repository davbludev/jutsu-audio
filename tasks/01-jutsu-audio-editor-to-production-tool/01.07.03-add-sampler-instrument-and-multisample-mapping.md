Map project samples across notes/velocities with envelopes, pitch, loop modes, voice limits, and missing-asset handling.

## Acceptance

Instrument state is portable and deterministic.

## Notes

- 2026-08-19: a sampler is an asset source, not an extension — what it plays is audio the project
  already holds, so it renders in `crates/jutsu-audio-engine/src/sampler.rs`, where the loader that
  fetches assets lives. Its whole state is the zones plus envelope and voice limit, which is what
  makes it portable: no instance state, nothing outside the project file.
- 2026-08-19: zones map pitch and velocity ranges to a sample with a root pitch (playback ratio),
  a gain, and either one-shot or looped playback. Velocity layers are how a kit gets its dynamics.
- 2026-08-19: the voice limit drops a newcomer rather than cutting a sounding voice — an offline
  render lays each note down complete, so there is nothing to cut. Predictable, and identical every
  render.
- 2026-08-19: a zone whose asset cannot be read reports a diagnostic and plays silence; the rest of
  the instrument still sounds. A zone naming an asset that does not exist is refused at the surface,
  before it can be stored.
