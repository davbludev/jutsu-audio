Expose list/describe/run/preview generator commands with JSON schemas and explicit seeds.

## Acceptance

Codex can discover capabilities without prose scraping and golden seeds reproduce across runs.

## Notes

- 2026-08-19: `list_extensions`, `describe_generator`, `preview_generator` and `run_generator`.
  Discovery carries parameter types, defaults, bounds and presets, so an agent can drive a
  generator it has never seen without scraping documentation.
- 2026-08-19: preview renders without a project and answers with peak, RMS and a sample
  fingerprint; `tests/generator_reproducibility.rs` pins a golden seed per generator and asserts
  the fingerprint is stable, that a different seed or parameter changes it, and that two exports
  of one project are identical.
- 2026-08-19: `run_generator` derives its asset and clip IDs from the recipe, which is what makes
  `replace` idempotent — a rerun replaces in place rather than piling up copies — and what keeps a
  `new` variant reproducible under its variant number.
