Two gaps that a mix hits immediately:

- The compressor hears only its own input, so a bass cannot duck under a kick.
- Routing is a single output per strip, so there is no way to send a copy of a signal somewhere
  and keep the dry one.

Sidechain keys and parallel sends. Both are project state, so both migrate.

## Acceptance

A key signal measurably ducks the strip that names it; a send reaches its destination without
removing the source from its own output; feedback loops in routing are refused by validation
rather than heard.

## Notes

- 2026-08-19: Sidechain keys done — `EffectInsert::sidechain`, `Effect::process_with_key`,
  key tracks rendered before the strips that name them, `set_effect_sidechain` on the CLI,
  proven by `crates/jutsu-audio-engine/tests/sidechain.rs`. Parallel sends are not started.
- 2026-08-19: Parallel sends done — `SendRoute` on a track, summed into their destination bus
  before any bus folds, `set_track_sends` on the CLI, proven by
  `crates/jutsu-audio-engine/tests/sends.rs`. Sends come from tracks only; a bus cannot send.
