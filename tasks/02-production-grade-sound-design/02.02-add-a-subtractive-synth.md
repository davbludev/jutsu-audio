`builtin.oscillator` is a waveform, a gain and a linear attack/release: the tone is identical
from the first frame of a note to the last. `builtin.subtractive` adds what makes a synthesiser
one — a full ADSR, a resonant low-pass with its own envelope and depth, unison voices with
detune and stereo spread, and velocity routed to brightness as well as level.

The inspector needs no new code: `synth_panel` builds itself from the descriptor.

## Acceptance

Registered, conformance-tested, reproducible; a held note changes timbre over its length; the
CLI can discover and drive every parameter without prose.
